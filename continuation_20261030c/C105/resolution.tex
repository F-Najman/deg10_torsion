\begin{proposition}\label{prop:decic-no105}
An elliptic curve over a number field of degree $10$ has no point of
order $105$.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Put $X=X_1(105)$ and $h=\langle22\rangle$. The exact winding
calculation has weighted dimension $289=g(X)$. Its only deficient
even-character orbit has conductor $21$. Since $22\equiv1\pmod{21}$,
the image $(h-1)J_X(\mathbb Q)$ is finite. We do not assume that
$J_X(\mathbb Q)$ has rank zero.

Suppose that $D$ is the rational trace divisor of a level structure
defined over a field of degree $10$. Thus $D$ is effective, entirely
noncuspidal and of degree $10$, with repetitions allowed. The complete
finite-field enumeration gives no noncuspidal places of degree at
most $9$ and $144$ places of degree $10$ on $X_{\mathbb F_2}$.
The latter form six orbits under the projective diamond group of
order $24$. Hence $\overline D$ is either entirely cuspidal or one
of these degree-$10$ places.

First suppose that $\overline D$ is cuspidal. Lift it to a cuspidal
divisor $C$ of degree $10$ over $\mathbb Q(\zeta_{105})$, which is
unramified at $2$. Let $c_0$ be a rational Tate cusp. The class
\[
 (h-1)[D-C]=(h-1)[D-10c_0]+(h-1)[10c_0-C]
\]
is torsion, by the finite-image assertion and Manin--Drinfeld. It
reduces to zero. The kernel of reduction on torsion over an unramified
dyadic field is killed by $2$, so
\[
                    2(hD-D-hC+C)\sim0.
\]
The positive degree of this divisor is at most $40$. However,
Abramovich's bound~\cite{Abramovich} gives
\[
 \operatorname{gon}_{\mathbb C}X
 \geq\frac7{800}\,4608=\frac{1008}{25}>40.
\]
Thus the divisor is zero. Comparing noncuspidal parts gives $hD=D$.

The action of $h$ on noncuspidal points is free of order $4$.
Indeed, $22^2\equiv64\pmod{105}$, and the three nontrivial powers
have projective orders $4,2,4$. A curve with $j\ne0,1728$ has no
nontrivial projective automorphism. At $j=0$ the projective
automorphism group has order $3$, so cannot give such a stabilizer.
At $j=1728$ a nontrivial stabilizer would require
$(22^k)^2\equiv-1\pmod{105}$, whereas these squares are $64,1,64$.
It follows that $4$ divides $\deg D=10$, a contradiction.

It remains to consider noncuspidal reduction. Let
$Z=X_1(35)$ and push $D$ forward under
\[
                  (E,P)\longmapsto(E,3P).
\]
Denote the resulting divisor by $F$. The Jacobian $J_Z(\mathbb Q)$
has rank zero by \cite[Theorem~3.1(2)]{DEHMZ}. On the point-level model
write $\delta=\langle3\rangle$. Eichler--Shimura and injectivity of
rational torsion reduction at the odd good prime $3$ give
\[
                     (T_3-1-3\delta)J_Z(\mathbb Q)=0.
\]
The rational Tate cusp satisfies the literal identity
$T_3c_0=c_0+3\delta c_0$. Applying the operator to $[F-10c_0]$
therefore shows that
\[
                         T_3F-F-3\delta F\sim0.        \tag{105.1}
\]

For each of the six source representatives, we compute its image
$P$ and $\delta P$ on the smooth genus-$25$ curve
$Z_{\mathbb F_2}$. Both have degree $10$ and norm multiplicity $1$.
The four cyclic $3$-subgroups form one rational orbit and one orbit
of length $3$. Their transported level points give target places of
degrees $10$ and $30$, both with norm multiplicity $1$. All inverse
places are computed with their full finite or infinite prime ideals.
In each case the exact Riemann--Roch calculation gives
\[
              h^0_Z(T_3P-P-3\delta P)=0.               \tag{105.2}
\]
This contradicts the specialization of (105.1).

The six tests are retained in \path{quotient35_RR.m}. A fresh native
replay reconstructs all $24$ prime ideals, verifies the source and
diamond points, and repeats all six calculations; its output is
\path{parent_native_RR.m}. Independently, all twelve kernel orbits
are reconstructed in Sage. The normalized isogeny coordinates agree
with the explicit V\'{e}lu sums over the two nonzero kernel points.
The source has exact order $105$, its transported level has exact
order $35$, and every target is bound to the native prime by an
intrinsic two-coordinate residue-field key. Thus the tests include
the actual transported levels and exact norm weights. The independent
source census and these controls are recorded in
\path{inputs/parent_source_census.json} and
\path{independent_sage_velu.json}.
\end{proof}
