# C91 supersingular degree-eight controls

This directory supplies finite marked-point controls for the separate C91 proof. It makes no degree-ten exclusion claim by itself.

`inputs/records91_8.m` pins both eligible supersingular F256 curves, each with cyclic group of order 273 and a point P of order 91. The source records and their original accepted CM action checks are copied and hash pinned without editing earlier-degree files.

`CM_level7_binding.m` and `independent_CM_level7_binding.json` prove the useful quotient binding. On each retained equation `y^2+a3*y=x^3`, choose a primitive cube root ζ with `(ζ*x(P),y(P))=16P`. For `P7=13P` and `S13=7P`, the same actual automorphism sends P7 to 2P7 and S13 to 3S13. The entire degree-six kernel polynomial of the cyclic subgroup generated by S13 is invariant. Thus every image on `X_1(7) × X_0(13)` is geometrically fixed by the point-level diamond `<2 mod7>`. Since the rational order-91 group is cyclic, checking these generators also covers every order-91 level structure on the two source twists. The two independent producers use native Magma and Sage point arithmetic.

If the separate genus-23 model has exactly two quadratic fixed places, its degree-eight source points therefore push to those places with norm four. The fixed-locus completeness and the relative formal-immersion ranks belong to the separate geometry packet, not to this directory.

`Hecke_CM_{3,5,11}.m` are exact diagnostics for the initially suggested Hecke/gonality shortcut. Each file contains the three source closed-point keys, their diamond-nine permutation, and all source/Hecke keys, orbit lengths, norm multiplicities and aggregated coefficients for `(diamond9-1)A_l P` and its diamond81 variant. The point-level annihilator is `A_l=T_l-1-l*diamond_l`.

- For l=3, the targets have degrees 8 and 24, both norm one; the resulting positive and negative degrees are 56.
- For l=5, there are two targets of degree 24, both norm one; the resulting degrees are 88.
- For l=11, there are four targets of degree 24, all norm one; the resulting degrees are 160.

These values hold for all three source supports and both diamond variants. The marked transports do not stay on the three degree-eight supports, so these tests do not give the proposed gonality contradiction below degree 40.

All keys are intrinsic two-coordinate keys `[degree,chosen_binary_linear_combination,minpoly,coordinate_vectors]` of the regular Tate modular units. They identify closed places independently of the finite-field representation. The initial Magma scope/parser diagnostic is preserved; its actual exit zero is not a completed certificate. Successful jobs require their explicit completion marker. All controls have exited and no worker or launcher is retained.
