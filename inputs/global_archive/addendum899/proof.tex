\begin{proposition}\label{prop:nonic-no899}
An elliptic curve over a number field of degree $9$ has no point of order $899$.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
The complete winding computation for $J_1(899)$ has total dimension
$32761=g(X_1(899))$. All character components have full winding submodule
except possibly characters with orders in
$\{1\}$.
The diamond $\langle3\rangle$ has projective order $420$, coprime
to every nontrivial exceptional character order. Thus each exceptional
character is trivial at $3$, and Kato's theorem gives that
$(\langle3\rangle-1)J_1(899)(\mathbb Q)$ is finite.
The high-order character components are certified by full-rank $T_2$
Krylov matrices modulo splitting primes. Every coefficient denominator
is invertible at the chosen prime; hence full rank holds in characteristic zero.
The complete dimension sum, the character evaluations, and every serialized
Krylov matrix are checked independently in the accompanying certificate.

Apply \cite[Proposition~4.4]{DN} at $q=3$, with $k_3=7$.
The exact modular index is $I_{899}=403200$, and
\[
 9k_3=63<\frac{325\cdot403200}{32768}
       \leq\operatorname{gon}_{\mathbb C}X_1(899).
\]
The least degree of a CM point is $1680>9$.
The trace divisor of a hypothetical degree-nine torsion point therefore
has non-CM support and is stable under this diamond.
Its free action would force $420\mid9$, a contradiction.
\end{proof}
