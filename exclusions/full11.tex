\begin{proposition}\label{prop:no-full11}
No elliptic curve over a number field of degree $10$ has full
$11$-torsion.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
The Weil pairing forces the field to be $K=\mathbb Q(\zeta_{11})$.
The curve $X_1(11)$ is isomorphic to
\[
 A:v^2+v=u^3-u^2.
\]
For the model $x^2y-xy^2+y-1=0$, the isomorphism on affine opens is
$u=1/y$, $v=x/y-1$.

We first prove that $A(K)$ has rank zero. The curve $A$ has conductor
$11$ and is non-CM, since its rational $j$-invariant is not integral.
Every twist of its newform by a character of
$\operatorname{Gal}(K/\mathbb Q)$ is non-CM and has level dividing $121$.
Indeed a nontrivial such character is tamely ramified only at $11$;
twisting the Steinberg representation there gives conductor exponent~$2$.
The complete archived newform lists of levels $1,11,121$ show that
every non-CM form has a proved nonzero central $L$-value.
Kato's rank-zero theorem, applied to these Dirichlet twists, therefore
gives $\operatorname{rank}A(K)=0$.
The positive-rank CM form of level $121$ does not contribute to these twists.

The prime $23$ splits completely in $K$. Good reduction at a prime
above $23$ gives $\#A(\mathbb F_{23})=25$, so $\#A(K)$ divides $25$.
A cusp gives a point $Q$ with $25Q=0$ and $5Q\ne0$.
Consequently $A(K)=\langle Q\rangle$ has exactly $25$ points.
Of these, ten are cusps. At the other fifteen points, evaluation of
the universal curve gives the three $j$-invariants
\[
 -121,\qquad-32768,\qquad-24729001.
\]
For each point, the certified universal equation has good reduction
at the prime above $23$ specified by $\zeta_{11}\mapsto2$.
The reduction orders are $22$ or $33$. Reduction is injective on
$11$-torsion, whereas $121$ divides neither order. Hence none of the
fifteen noncuspidal points represents a curve with full $11$-torsion.
\end{proof}

The certificate \path{full11_generator.json} preserves $Q$.
A separate Sage computation checks its order, enumerates all $25$ points,
evaluates the universal family, and recounts every reduction.
The rank input and the complete non-CM newform list used in the argument
are recorded in \path{full11_rank_input.json} and the archived cache.
