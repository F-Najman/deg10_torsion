\begin{lemma}\label{lem:hilbert}
Let $F$ be a number field and let $X_1(m,n)$ denote a fixed Weil-pairing
component over $F$. Suppose that its universal elliptic curve has full
torsion $C_m\times C_n$ over $F(X_1(m,n))$. If $X_1(m,n)$ has an
$F$-rational function of degree $r$, then $C_m\times C_n$ occurs as
full torsion over a field of degree $r[F:\mathbb Q]$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Hilbert irreducibility gives irreducible fibres of degree $r$.
By uniform boundedness, only finitely many additional torsion orders
can occur in fields of degree $r[F:\mathbb Q]$. For each order, use
the finite covers which specify a torsion point outside the indicated
subgroup. Their generic degrees exceed one. Apply Hilbert irreducibility
simultaneously to their compositions with the function. No such fibre
acquires an additional rational torsion point. Removing the cusps and
the finitely many degenerate fibres proves the assertion.
\end{proof}

We apply this lemma over $F=\mathbb Q$ for $m\leq2$ and over
$F=\mathbb Q(\zeta_m)$ for $m=3,4,6$. The standard universal family
has exactly the specified generic torsion. The following functions
are certified by principal cuspidal divisors:
\[
\begin{array}{c|l|c}
m&n&\deg f\\\hline
1&23,26,27,32,33,34,36&10\\
2&20,22,24&10\\
3&9,12&5\\
4&8,12&5\\
6&6&5
\end{array}
\]
The computation uses the integral modular-symbol period lattice in
\texttt{mdsage}. A degree-zero cuspidal divisor belongs to the
principal lattice precisely when its Abel--Jacobi image vanishes.
For each row, the certificate records a Galois-invariant vector in
this lattice whose positive part has the indicated degree. A rational
principal divisor descends to a rational function by Hilbert's theorem~90.
For $m>2$ we use the standard isomorphism, over $\mathbb Q(\zeta_m)$,
with the $\Gamma_H(mn)$ model, where
$H=\langle-1,1+n,\ldots,1+(m-1)n\rangle$.
Here $n$ denotes the second invariant factor; the level is $mn$.

The vectors, ordered cusps, lattice bases, and the exact software revision
are in the unit certificates in the occurrence directory.
The software revision is pinned in \path{shared/mdsage_manifest.json}. The independent replay recomputes
lattice membership and degrees for all fifteen vectors. Initial probes
which merely compared genus and degree are not used in this proof.
