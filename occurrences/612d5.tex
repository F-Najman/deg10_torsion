\subsection{A sporadic occurrence of \texorpdfstring{$C_6\times C_{12}$}{C6 x C12}}
\begin{proposition}\label{prop:decic612}
The group $C_6\times C_{12}$ occurs as the full torsion group
of an elliptic curve over a decic number field.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Let $F=\Q(z)$, where $z^2-z+1=0$, and let $w$ be a root of
\[
 3w^5+(4-5z)w^4+(7-8z)w^3-7(z+1)w^2
       +6(z-1)w+2z-1=0.
\]
This polynomial is irreducible over $F$. Put $K=F(w)$, so
$[K:\Q]=10$, and define
\[
 r=\frac{w^2+1}{w^2-w},\qquad
 s=\frac{w^2+w+1}{w^2}.
\]
Consider
\[
 E:\quad y^2+(1+s-rs)xy+rs(1-r)y
       =x^3+rs(1-r)x^2.
\]
The point $Q=(0,0)$ has order $12$.
An independent point $P$ of order $6$ is given by
\[
\begin{aligned}
2x(P)={}&(25z+10)w^4+(45-20z)w^3+(79-17z)w^2\\
 &+(22-109z)w+39z-46,\\
2y(P)={}&(222z+237)w^4+(651-537z)w^3+(1012-601z)w^2\\
 &-(1518z+223)w+808z-861.
\end{aligned}
\]
Exact arithmetic verifies $e_6(P,2Q)=z$. Hence
$C_6\times C_{12}\subseteq E(K)_{\rm tors}$.

The prime specified by $z\mapsto14$, $w\mapsto8$ has residue
field $\mathbb F_{61}$ and is unramified; both defining
polynomials have simple roots. The curve has good reduction
\[
 \overline E:\quad y^2-4xy+4y=x^3+4x^2,
 \qquad \#\overline E(\mathbb F_{61})=72.
\]
Torsion injectivity gives $\#E(K)_{\rm tors}\le72$, proving equality.
Sage independently verifies the irreducible field, both point
orders, the Weil pairing and the reduction count, including a
separate count of all $61$ Weierstrass fibers.

The associated point on $X_1(6,12)$ has degree five over $F$.
The Jacobian has rank zero by \cite[Theorem~4.1]{DS}, and its
$F$-gonality is at least six by \cite[Table~1]{NV}.
Thus there are only finitely many such degree-five points,
and this occurrence is sporadic.
\end{proof}
