\begin{proposition}\label{prop:octic45}
No elliptic curve over an octic number field has a point of order $45$.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Put $X=X_1(45)$. Its genus is $41$ and its rational Jacobian has rank
zero by \cite[Theorem~3.1(2)]{DEHMZ}. Let $D$ be the rational effective
divisor of degree $8$ obtained from a hypothetical octic moduli point,
with multiplicities if its field of definition has smaller degree.

The complete noncuspidal closed-place counts on $X_{\mathbb F_2}$
through degree $8$ are
\[
 (0,0,0,0,0,0,0,60).
\]
They agree with an independent Frobenius similarity-class count.
Thus any noncuspidal part of $\overline D$ is one octic place, with
no cuspidal complement. The $60$ places have five diamond orbits.
For each representative $P$, the fresh Hecke calculation finds a
prime $q\in\{7,11,13,17,19\}$ for which
\[
 (T_q-1-q\langle q\rangle)P
\]
is nonprincipal. This contradicts Lemma~\ref{lem:octic-hecke}.

It remains to consider entirely cuspidal reduction. The cusp degrees
over $\mathbb F_2$ are $1^{12},2^4,4^5,6^2,12$.
We apply Lemma~\ref{lem:quotient-Cartier} directly to $X$.
The curve has good reduction at $2$, a rational cusp, an etale
cyclotomic cusp scheme, and the usual Eichler--Shimura operators.
The space
\[
 U=(\mathcal C-1)H^0(X_{\mathbb F_2},\Omega^1)
\]
has dimension $39$. The fresh degree-eight computation constructs all
$124$ local restriction matrices at the $23$ cusps of degree at most
$8$. On every one of the $148945$ effective cuspidal divisors of
degree $8$, restriction from $U$ has rank $8$.

The exporter records the original-basis local expansions and verifies
their precision and holomorphy. At each cusp, division by one common
local unit changes none of the restriction ranks. Independent binary
linear algebra verifies the Cartier image, all $124$ projected
restriction matrices, and the complete enumeration with zero rank
exceptions. As a separate geometric check, all $103$ previously
computed exact differential-space kernels within this precision
range agree with the fresh expansions. These are local geometric
data, not an exclusion imported from another degree. The formal
immersion criterion excludes entirely cuspidal reduction and proves
the proposition.
\end{proof}
