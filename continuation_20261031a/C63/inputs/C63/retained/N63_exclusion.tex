\begin{proposition}\label{prop:octic63}
No elliptic curve over an octic number field has a point of order $63$.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Put $Y=X_1(63)/\langle8\rangle$. The diamond $8$ acts as $-1$ on the
$9$-component and as $1$ on the $7$-component. It acts freely also in
characteristic $2$. At a noncuspidal fixed point the corresponding
automorphism of the elliptic curve has square $1$, hence is $1$ or $-1$;
neither induces the two different signs. At a cusp, the primitive-column
description likewise requires one common sign at $3$ and $7$, which is
impossible. Thus the quotient is etale and $Y$ has good reduction at $2$.
Its genus is $49$.

The Jacobian $J(Y)(\mathbb Q)$ has rank zero. Exact modular symbols
show that each even character $\chi$ modulo $63$ with $\chi(8)=1$
has a full winding submodule. There are five character orbits, and
their dimensions weighted by $\varphi(\operatorname{ord}\chi)$ sum
to $49$. Kato's theorem applies to every simple factor of $J(Y)$.

We use the direct model
\[
 E_9:y^2+(1-c)xy-by=x^3-bx^2,\qquad
 c=t^2(t-1),\quad b=t^2(t-1)(t^2-t+1),
\]
and adjoin a root $u$ of $\psi_7(E_9)$. The point $(0,0)$ has exact
order $9$, and $u=x(P_7)$ forgets the sign of the $7$-point, as required
for the quotient by $\langle8\rangle$. The polynomial has degree $24$
and is irreducible. Its function field has genus $49$ and $j$-degree
$864$. Its closed cusps at $2$ have degrees $1^9,2^3,3^9,6$.
All nine rational cusps at $2$ lift to rational cusps over $\mathbb Q$.
The primitive-column audit verifies both the freeness at the cusps
and this rational-lift assertion.

Let $D_X$ be the rational degree-eight trace divisor associated to
a hypothetical point of order $63$. The complete finite-field count
on $X_1(63)$ has no noncuspidal places of degrees $1$ through $6$ at
$2$, and has $54$ noncuspidal places in each degree $7$ and $8$.
Consequently, if $\overline D_X$ has a noncuspidal part, it consists
either of a degree-seven place and a rational cusp, or of a degree-eight
place.

The direct quotient enumeration gives respectively $189$ and $216$
pairs $(t,u)$ over $\mathbb F_{128}$ and $\mathbb F_{256}$ which lift
to source points. In each case these give $27$ closed points of the
indicated degree, in three orbits under the residual diamonds $2,5$.
The enumeration checks the exact Frobenius degree of every pair and
the existence of the $7$-point itself over the residue field.
For a representative $P$ of each of these six orbits, compute
\[
 A_5P=T_5P-P-5\langle5\rangle P.
\]
All six divisors are nonprincipal. These are exact function-field
calculations: the six $5$-isogenies transport both torsion points,
Tate normalization recovers the new parameter and $7$-coordinate,
and prime ideals identify the resulting places. The divisor
multiplicities include the ratios of residue degrees.

Since $J(Y)(\mathbb Q)$ has rank zero, $A_5$ annihilates its rational
points, by reduction at $5$ and Eichler--Shimura. It also annihilates
each rational cusp as a divisor. The rational cusp complement in the
degree-seven case therefore contributes zero. Both noncuspidal
reduction cases contradict the six tests.

It remains to exclude entirely cuspidal reduction. Push $D_X$ forward
to a noncuspidal rational effective divisor $D$ of degree $8$ on $Y$.
The quotient Cartier criterion applies: $Y$ has good reduction and a
rational cusp, its cusp fields are cyclotomic, and $T_2,\langle2\rangle$
descend and satisfy Eichler--Shimura. The Cartier-difference space
\[
 U=(\mathcal C-1)H^0(Y_{\mathbb F_2},\Omega^1)
\]
has dimension $45$. The fresh calculation computes all $103$ spaces
$H^0(\Omega^1(-mP))$ at the $22$ cusps, for
$1\leq m\leq\lfloor8/\deg(P)\rfloor$. Their annihilators give
compatible restriction matrices. On each of the $44046$ effective
cuspidal divisors of degree $8$, restriction from $U$ has rank $8$.
Independent binary elimination verifies all $103$ kernel equalities,
all $103$ Cartier projections, and all $44046$ rank-eight assertions.
The criterion therefore forces $D$ to be cuspidal, a contradiction.
\end{proof}
