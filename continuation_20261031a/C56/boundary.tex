\begin{lemma}
Let $D$ be an effective rational divisor of degree $10$ on
$X=X_1(56)$ supported outside the cusps.
Its reduction at 3 cannot be entirely cuspidal.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The Jacobian $J(\mathbb Q)$ has rank zero
\cite[Theorem~3.1(2)]{DEHMZ}, and $\operatorname{gon}_{\mathbb Q}X\geq 7\cdot1152/800>10$.
The curve has good reduction at the odd prime $p=3$.
Suppose that $\overline D$ is cuspidal. The finite etale cuspidal
boundary gives an effective lift $C$ over $\mathbb Q_p$ of degree
$10$ with this reduction. Choose a rational cusp $c_0$.
The class $[D-10c_0]$ is torsion by rank zero, and
$[C-10c_0]$ is torsion by Manin--Drinfeld. Hence
$[D-C]\in J(\mathbb Q_p)$ is torsion and reduces to zero.
The kernel of reduction on $J(\mathbb Q_p)$ is torsion-free
for odd $p$, so $D\sim C$ over $\mathbb Q_p$.
These are distinct effective divisors, whence
$h^0(X_{\mathbb Q_p},D)\geq2$. Base change gives
$h^0(X,D)\geq2$, contradicting the gonality bound.
\end{proof}
