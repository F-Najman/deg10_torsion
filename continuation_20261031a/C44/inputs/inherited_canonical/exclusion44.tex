\begin{proposition}\label{prop:nonic-no44}
There is no point of order $44$ on an elliptic curve over a number
field of degree $9$.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Put $X=X_1(44)$ and $J=J_1(44)$. The curve has genus $36$,
$J(\mathbb Q)$ is finite by \cite{DEHMZ}, and
$\operatorname{gon}_{\mathbb Q}(X)\geq10$ by
\cite[Table~1]{NV}. Let $D$ be the degree-$9$ trace divisor of a
hypothetical noncuspidal point.

The divisor $D$ cannot have entirely cuspidal reduction at $3$.
Indeed, such a reduction lifts to a cuspidal divisor $C$ of degree
$9$ over a finite extension of $\mathbb Q_3$ unramified at $3$.
With a rational cusp $c_0$, both $[D-9c_0]$ and $[C-9c_0]$ are
torsion, by rank zero and Manin--Drinfeld, respectively.
Thus $[D-C]$ is torsion and reduces to zero. Torsion reduction is
injective at an unramified odd prime, so $D\sim C$ over this local
field. Since $D\ne C$, base change gives $h^0(X,D)\geq2$, contrary
to the gonality bound.

At $3$, the noncuspidal closed-place counts in degrees $1$ through
$9$ are
\[
 (0,0,0,0,60,160,240,920,2260).
\]
These counts are independently obtained from the exact Frobenius
polynomial. Hence the noncuspidal part of $\overline D$ is a single
place $P$ of degree $5$, $6$, $7$, $8$ or $9$.
The finite cusp signature is $1^{15}2^5 5^3 10$.
Every cusp of degree at most $4$ is the reduction of a rational
closed cuspidal divisor of the same degree. In particular every
cuspidal complement for the first four degrees satisfies
\[
 (T_q-1-q\langle q\rangle)C'=0
\]
for every good odd prime $q$ used in the finite tests.
There are $6$, $16$, $24$, and $92$ diamond orbits in these four
degrees. The retained exact Hecke tests give a nonprincipal divisor
for each of these $138$ representatives. The new complement audit
through degree $4$ proves that the same tests exclude them here.
Thus only pure degree-$9$ noncuspidal reduction remains.

Since $J(\mathbb Q)$ is torsion, Eichler--Shimura and torsion
injectivity at the good prime $5$ give
\[
 A_5J(\mathbb Q)=0,\qquad A_5=T_5-1-5\langle5\rangle.
\]
The same operator kills a rational cusp as an actual divisor.
Consequently $A_5D$ is principal, and its reduction would be
principal. The $2260$ degree-$9$ places form $226$ diamond orbits.
For each representative put
\[
 B=T-U,\qquad T=T_5P,\qquad U=P+5\langle5\rangle P.
\]
Both $T$ and $U$ have degree $54$. We show that $\ell(B)=0$ in
all $226$ cases.

We construct the canonical model over $\mathbb F_3$ from an exact
basis of holomorphic differentials on the degree-$19$ function-field
model of $X$. Its $561$ quadratic relations define a curve of degree
$70$ and genus $36$ in $\mathbb P^{35}$. Put
$R_i=H^0(X_{\mathbb F_3},iK)$. Exact reduction in its coordinate
ring gives the multiplication tensor
\[
 R_2\otimes R_2\longrightarrow R_4,
 \qquad \dim R_2=105,\quad\dim R_4=245.
\]
The products of canonical coordinates have rank $105$, so the
computed basis of $R_2$ is complete. Since $\deg(2K)=140\geq2g+1$,
the line bundle $2K$ is normally generated. The $245$ standard
monomials in degree $4$ therefore identify the computed target with
the full space $R_4$. All $11025$ products are retained; symmetry
requires only $5565$ normal-form calculations.

The complete $5$-isogeny kernel partition gives $T$, including its
closed-place degrees and norm multiplicities. The divisor $U$ is
computed by actual multiplication of the level point by $5$.
At their supports, the plane equation and the formal implicit-function
theorem give local parameters to the required multiplicities.
Evaluation of the canonical coordinates gives matrices
$E_T,E_U$ of size $105\times54$, both of rank $54$.
For each case we retain nonzero sections
\[
 F,H\in R_2(-T),\qquad \dim R_2(-T)=51.
\]
Let $M_F,M_H$ be their multiplication matrices from $R_2$ to $R_4$.
The matrix $M_F$ has rank $105$. We retain a matrix $Q$ of size
$245\times140$ with $M_FQ=0$, and verify
\[
 \operatorname{rank}[\,E_U\mid M_HQ\,]=105.
\]
Lemma~\ref{lem:canonical-multiplication-sieve}, with $L=M=2K$,
therefore gives $\ell(T-U)=0$.

Independent arithmetic reconstructs every restriction matrix from
the saved local jets and checks the section identities, product
annihilators, and all $226$ ranks. The source enumeration retains
$2260$ distinct degree-$9$ places, so the independently known count
proves coverage. An untouched native $5$-isogeny calculation checks
all six isogenies in one control case; an exact native
Riemann--Roch calculation on its degree-zero Hecke divisor also
gives dimension zero. Every new native computation used in this proof
has a recorded successful exit. Thus no pure degree-$9$ case is
principal, a contradiction.
\end{proof}
