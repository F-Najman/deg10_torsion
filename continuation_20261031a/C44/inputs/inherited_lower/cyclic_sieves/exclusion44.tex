\begin{proposition}\label{prop:octic44}
No elliptic curve over an octic number field has a point of order $44$.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
The rational Jacobian of $X_1(44)$ has rank zero by
\cite[Theorem~3.1(2)]{DEHMZ}. Its rational gonality is at least $10$,
as follows from the bound over $\mathbb F_3$ in \cite[Table~1]{NV}.
Lemma~\ref{lem:octic-odd-cuspidal} therefore excludes entirely
cuspidal reduction at $3$.

The complete noncuspidal closed-place counts over $\mathbb F_3$
through degree $8$ are
\[
 (0,0,0,0,60,160,240,920).
\]
An independent Frobenius similarity-class computation gives the same
counts. Hence the noncuspidal part of a degree-eight reduction is a
single place of degree $5$, $6$, $7$ or $8$.
The cusp degrees at $3$ are $1^{15},2^5,5^3,10$.
For every possible cusp complement the complete cusp-orbit test
permits $q\in\{5,7,13,17,19\}$.

The fresh degree-eight Hecke sieve tests respectively $6$, $16$,
$24$ and $92$ diamond representatives in those four degrees.
Every one of these $138$ cases has a nonprincipal $A_q$ image for
some permitted $q$. The completed calculation covers all $66$
partitions through degree $8$, with zero survivors; the other
partitions have empty noncuspidal support sets.
Lemma~\ref{lem:octic-hecke} excludes noncuspidal reduction as well.
The exact log and independent coverage audit are
\texttt{N44\_p3\_smart.log} and \texttt{N44\_audit.json}.
\end{proof}
