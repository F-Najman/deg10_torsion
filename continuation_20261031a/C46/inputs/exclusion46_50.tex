\begin{proposition}\label{prop:nonic-no46-50}
An elliptic curve over a number field of degree $9$ has no point of
order $46$ or $50$.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
For $N=46,50$, the rational Jacobian of $X_1(N)$ has rank zero by
\cite[Theorem~3.1(2)]{DEHMZ}, and its rational gonality is at least $10$
by \cite[Table~1]{NV}. Lemma~\ref{lem:nonic-cont-odd-cuspidal}, with
$d=9$ and $p=3$, excludes entirely cuspidal reduction.

The noncuspidal closed-place counts over $\mathbb F_3$, through degree
$9$, are respectively
\[
 (0,0,0,55,44,55,264,781,1958),\qquad
 (0,0,0,0,0,60,200,1045,2040).
\]
Independent counts using Frobenius similarity classes give the same
numbers. The finite cusp signatures are $1^{22},11^2$ and
$1^{20},4^4,10^2$. In both cases the displayed finite cusp orbits have
the same degrees as their global cyclotomic orbits.
Thus every cuspidal complement of a nonempty support, of degree at most
$5$ for $N=46$ and at most $3$ for $N=50$, is rationally liftable.
In the second case all these complements are supported on rational cusps.
The exact cusp-field calculation verifies that the permitted primes are
$5,7,11,13,17,19$ for $N=46$ and $7,11,13,17,19$ for $N=50$.

Use the maps $X_1(46)\to X_1(23)$ and $X_1(50)\to X_1(25)$
which double the level point. The two target rational Jacobians are
cuspidally generated, of orders $408991$ and $227555$, by
\cite[Corollary~4.14 and Table~4]{DEHMZ}.
At $3$ their exact finite degree-zero class groups are cyclic of orders
$9406793$ and $2503105$. The quotients by the global rational cuspidal
images are $C_{23}$ and $C_{11}$. Every effective target cuspidal
complement of degree at most $9$ has zero class in the respective
quotient. We apply Lemma~\ref{lem:nonic-cont-quotient-cusps}, including
residue-field multiplicities in every pushforward, then the permitted
$A_q$ tests on the source when needed.

For $N=46$ the complete support types are
\[
 (4),(5),(6),(7),(8),(4,4),(9),(5,4).
\]
The test counts, after applying diamonds to the first support, are
\[
 5,4,5,24,71,275,178,220.
\]
For the mixed types, the other support ranges through all $55$ quartic
places; repetitions are included. For $N=50$ the support types are
$(6),(7),(8),(9)$ and the counts are $6,20,107,204$.
All $782$ and $337$ tests are negative.

The computations for supports of degree at most $8$ are immutable
finite-field calculations from the preceding project. Their transfer
uses no exclusion in another number-field degree: the supports,
Hecke divisors and principality tests are unchanged, all the old primes
remain permitted for the larger complements, and the target quotient
complement classes remain zero. These conditions are checked afresh
in the degree-$9$ logs. The new degree-$9$ support types are exhausted
in separate complete native runs, each with exit code $0$.
The independent coverage audit enumerates all $96$ integer partitions
of degrees one through nine. Untouched native isogeny calculations
also reproduce the fast Hecke divisor on a source support for each level. No nonempty support remains, proving the proposition.
\end{proof}
