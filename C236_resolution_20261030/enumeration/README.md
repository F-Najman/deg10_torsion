# C2 × C36: finite census and genus-seven quotient sieve

Completed first stage, 2026-10-30. This directory supplies the noncuspidal-support input to the subsequent genus-nine and X1(36) sieves in `../geometry/`. It does not by itself exclude the group; 1251 support patterns remain after this stage. The separate all-cuspidal branch belongs to `../cuspidal_boundary/`.

## Source census

The source is X1(2,36), genus 41, at the good prime 5. Its cusp signature is 1^24 2^8 6^4. Newton identities applied to the independently computed degree-82 Frobenius polynomial give the noncuspidal closed-place counts in degrees 1,...,10:

`0, 0, 48, 120, 720, 2904, 10416, 50334, 218080, 979512`.

`source3.m` through `source9.m` are exact copies of accepted degree-independent census data from the degree-nine computation. `reuse_manifest.json` pins these seven files and four copied helpers, with source paths and SHA256 hashes. The independent integer replay checks every pinned copy.

`source10_s0.m` through `source10_s2.m` partition all 976887 Frobenius orbits of j-invariants over F_(5^10). They enumerate twists using full rational 2-torsion, halving to order 4, and the 9-division polynomial. Every stored source pair has order 36 and an independent point of order 2. The 24 level automorphisms and all ten Frobenius powers give canonical keys. The merged census has 40904 records: 30 of actual degree 5 and 40874 of actual degree 10. The separate counts 720 and 979512 agree exactly with the Frobenius polynomial. Only actual degree-10 records are used as degree-10 supports.

The source level transformations are

`(P,Q) -> (P + 18*b*Q, c*P + u*Q)`,

where b,c lie in {0,1} and u lies in {1,5,7,11,13,17}. Normalizing the first noncuspidal summand by these transformations leaves 2,5,30,126,434,2115,9088,40874 representatives in degrees 3,...,10.

## Two quotient maps and exact classes

Both maps have target Y=X1(2,18), genus 7:

1. `(E,P,Q) -> (E,P,2Q)`.
2. Quotient by `<18Q>`, retaining the images of P and Q.

`target18_proven_picard.m` is a fresh proven Picard presentation with finite invariants [6,84,252] and 63 factorbase places. The subgroup generated by all normalized cusp classes is H with invariants [2,42,126]; the quotient is C2 × C6, of order 12. The accepted rational-image theorem makes membership in H necessary. Every finite cusp class lies in H, so the cusp complement does not affect these two tests.

Every projected source place is normalized by the stored rational target cusp A and multiplied by the exact residue degree ratio. Class computation retains an explicit identity

`D = E - R - div(h)`

with both finite and infinite orders checked. The portable forward map does not set an unproved ClassGroup flag. Exact identities and the prime ideal bases are stored in `filter18_*_RR.log.witnesses.py` and `filter18_images_*.m`.

For degrees 8,9,10 the support is a single noncuspidal place, so a failure of the first map permits skipping the second. The eight degree-10 shards are disjoint by `(representativeIndex-1) mod 8`.

For degrees 3,...,7 both target classes are computed on every normalized representative. `target18_aut_actions.m` proves the induced actions of the three target level transformations on C2 × C6 using all 48 noncuspidal factorbase places; these places generate the quotient. Each transformed place remains in the factorbase, so this step needs no Riemann–Roch descent. `small_qclasses3.m` through `small_qclasses7.m` propagate the two quotient classes to every actual source place. The resulting counts are 48,120,720,2904,10416. Duplicate Frobenius keys have identical propagated classes. For degrees 3,...,6 the result also agrees with a separately computed full-place class list.

It independently checks Newton identities, the three-way source10 merge, all source-copy hashes, the exact quotient lattice index, the 48 automorphism action tests, source-key/field correspondence, and every one of the 135282 support decisions.
