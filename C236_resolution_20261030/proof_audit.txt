The source is X1(2,36), not X1(36). The former has genus 41,
rank-zero rational Jacobian, good reduction at 5, and F5-gonality at
least 10 (NV, Table 1). The latter is the genus-17 final sieve target.

The source enumeration includes every noncuspidal support of total
degree at most 10, including repeated supports. Normalizing one support
by a rational level automorphism is legitimate: all the quotient class
conditions also apply to the transformed global divisor. Each image of
a source place of degree d on a target place of degree e is counted with
multiplicity d/e. Cuspidal complements are retained. For the genus-7
and genus-9 quotients they have zero class in the necessary quotient;
for X1(36), every effective cusp complement of its actual remaining
degree is enumerated. No rational-cusp-only simplification is used there.

The exceptional oldN8 argument concerns any QQ-rational effective
divisor D of degree 10 with entirely noncuspidal global support, whether
irreducible, reducible, or repeated. Its reduction can have cuspidal
components. The cusp complement C has degree 2. On the order-four free
quotient, pi_*C is either one of two quadratic cusp places or a sum of
two of the six rational cusp places, with repetitions allowed. These
are the 2+21 Riemann--Roch tests. All four character dimensions are
(1,0,0,0). The orbit sum Omega has degree 8; the two equivalent divisors
D+Omega-C and gD+Omega-gC have degree 16. Rank zero and Manin--Drinfeld
make g[D-C]-[D-C] torsion. Its specialization vanishes, and unramified
odd-prime torsion injectivity makes it zero. Semicontinuity and h0=1
force equality of the effective divisors. Their cuspidal parts then
give C=gC, impossible for degree 2 with free cusp orbits of length 4.

The cuspidal-pencil calculation allows nonreduced fibres. If n distinct
rational cusps lie in a degree-10 fibre, its weighted geometric squared
norm is at most (11-n)^2+n-1, including any nonrational cusp components.
The two largest of six rational fibres contain enough of the 24 rational
cusps that their difference has norm at most 104. Its positive part has
degree 10. The full geometric principal cusp lattice, intersected with
Frobenius-5 invariants, has no such vector. Both the complete lattice
and short-vector enumeration were reconstructed independently; testing
only a chosen family of modular units would not suffice.

A rational degree-10 pencil specializes, using a saturated integral
section lattice, to independent sections. After removing base points,
its moving degree is at most 10. The F5-gonality bound forces degree
exactly 10 and removes all base points. The boundary lemma gives a
rational special fibre with some noncuspidal support. A QQ parameter
lifting it can be chosen away from the finitely many global cusp images.
The resulting global divisor is entirely noncuspidal and has the
forbidden reduction. Irreducibility is not required. Thus gon_Q>=11.
The shared rank-zero cuspidal-reduction lemma excludes a noncuspidal
trace divisor of degree 10 with entirely cuspidal reduction: it would
give h0(D)>=2 over an unramified local extension, hence over QQ by
cohomology base change, contradicting this gonality bound.
