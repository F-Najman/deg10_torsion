\begin{proposition}\label{prop:nonic-no81}
An elliptic curve over a number field of degree $9$ has no point of
order $81$.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Put $X=X_1(81)$. The exact winding computation gives full winding
submodule in every even-character component. The relative dimensions
and coefficient-field degrees give
\[
 4+18\cdot8+6\cdot6+2\cdot3=190=g(X).
\]
Thus $J_1(81)(\mathbb Q)$ has rank zero.

We compute rational $2$-torsion on the integral homology of the exact
Jacobian \texttt{JOne(81)}, with sign $0$. Its homology lattice has
rank $380$. For $q=5,7$, the endomorphism
\[
 A_q=T_q-1-q\langle q\rangle
\]
kills rational $2$-torsion, by Eichler--Shimura and injectivity of
torsion reduction at the odd good prime $q$.
On this same integral lattice, the diamond matrix is recovered from
$q\langle q\rangle=T_q^2-T_{q^2}$ before reduction modulo $2$.
The kernel of $A_5$ over $\mathbb F_2$ has dimension $8$, and its
intersection with the kernel of $A_7$ is zero. The exported integral
matrices are independently checked by binary linear algebra, and
a fresh native computation gives the same result. Hence
\[
 J_1(81)(\mathbb Q)[2]=0.
\]

The index of $\Gamma_1(81)$ in $\operatorname{PSL}_2(\mathbb Z)$
is $2916$. Abramovich's bound~\cite{Abramovich} gives
\[
 \operatorname{gon}_{\mathbb C}X
 \geq \frac{325}{32768}\,2916
 =\frac{236925}{8192}>27.
\]
A point of order $81$ over a field of degree $9$ gives a rational
effective noncuspidal divisor of degree $9$ on $X$. This contradicts
Lemma~\ref{lem:nonic81-Hecke-finite-support} with $d=9$.
\end{proof}
