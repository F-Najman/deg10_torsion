\begin{lemma}\label{lem:decic-318-orbit-relations}
Let $X/F$ be a smooth proper curve with finite Jacobian group
$J(F)$, let $c_0\in X(F)$, and let $g_1,\ldots,g_r$ be
$F$-automorphisms of $X$.
Let $b_1,\ldots,b_s\in J(F)$ be fixed classes.
Suppose that torsion specialization is injective at two primes
$\mathfrak p$ and $\mathfrak q$ of good reduction.
For an effective $F$-rational divisor $D$ of degree $d$, form
at each prime the ordered list
\[
 \overline b_1,\ldots,\overline b_s,
 [g_1\overline D-d\overline c_0],\ldots,
 [g_r\overline D-d\overline c_0].
\]
The two lists have the same integral relation lattice.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
All entries are specializations of the same ordered list of
classes in the finite group $J(F)$.
An integral linear combination specializes to zero if and only
if the corresponding global torsion class is zero.
Thus both relation lattices equal the kernel of the global map
from $\mathbb Z^{s+r}$ to $J(F)$.
\end{proof}
