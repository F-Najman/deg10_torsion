\begin{lemma}\label{lem:nonic48-class-filter}
Put $X=X_1(48)$ and $Y=X_1(2,24)$. There is a degree-$2$ map
$\pi:X\to Y$ over $\mathbb Q$. Write the reduction of a rational
effective degree-$9$ divisor on $X$ as $D_{\mathrm{nc}}+C$, with
$r=\deg D_{\mathrm{nc}}$ and $C$ cuspidal. Let $c_0$ be the chosen
rational cusp of $Y$. Then
\[
 -q[\pi_*D_{\mathrm{nc}}-rc_0]\in\mathcal C_{9-r},
\]
where $q:J_Y(\mathbb F_5)\to J_Y(\mathbb F_5)/H$ is the quotient
by the specialization image $H$ of $J_Y(\mathbb Q)$, and
\[
 \mathcal C_d=\{q[C'-dc_0]: C'\text{ is effective cuspidal of degree }d\}.
\]
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The map is
\[
 (E,P)\longmapsto
 (E/\langle24P\rangle,T_2,\overline P),
\]
where $T_2$ is the nonzero dual-kernel point. The image of $P$ has
order $24$ and is independent of $T_2$. The two lifts differ by
multiplication by $25$, so the map has degree $2$.
It extends to the compactifications and preserves cusps.
Proper pushforward includes the residue-degree multiplicity $1$ or $2$.

The target has genus $13$. Its Jacobian has rank $0$, since it is
a quotient of $J_X$. By \cite[Proposition~4.16 and Table~3]{DEHMZ},
\[
 J_Y(\mathbb Q)\simeq C_2\times C_4^2\times C_{120}\times C_{240}
\]
is generated by rational cuspidal divisors. The integral modular
units identify $19$ rational cuspidal blocks of degrees
$1^8,2^6,4^5$; their characteristic-zero identification is given by
the retained integral-unit lemma.

The proved finite Picard presentation gives
\[
 J_Y(\mathbb F_5)\simeq C_2^2\times C_4^2\times C_{12}
 \times C_{24}\times C_{480}^2.
\]
The block classes generate a subgroup $H$ of order $921600$,
which is the full rational image. The quotient has invariants
$[2,2,24,48]$ and order $4608$.
The class of the projected global divisor belongs to $H$.
Its cuspidal part supplies an element of $\mathcal C_{9-r}$,
which proves the condition.

We recompute every complement set for degree $9$. Their cardinalities
for $d=0,\ldots,9$ are
\[
 1,5,23,75,178,378,661,1065,1487,1859.
\]
All finite target cusps are allowed, so these sets contain every
actual projected complement.
\end{proof}

The sixteen rational automorphisms
\[
 T_2\longmapsto T_2+\epsilon12Q,\qquad
 Q\longmapsto aQ+\eta T_2,
 \quad \epsilon,\eta\in\{0,1\},\quad a\in\{1,5,7,11\}
\]
preserve $H$ and each $\mathcal C_d$. The normalization changes
by $9[g(c_0)-c_0]\in H$. Thus the condition is invariant under
these automorphisms for a single support, with its proper
pushforward multiplicity retained. The source image need not be
stable under this target action. Mixed supports are tested using
their actual classes; only simultaneous source diamonds are used.
